# Working & Learning Preferences

State: `STABLE` unless noted. Last updated: 2026-09-04.

## How Dalton Learns Best

Practical implementation, hands-on exercises, detailed-but-clear explanation, conceptual decomposition, troubleshooting, worked examples, understanding *mechanisms* rather than just getting answers, connecting theory to real systems.

Preferred progression for teaching technical material:

1. Concept
2. Mechanism
3. Example
4. Implementation
5. User attempt
6. Diagnosis
7. Application

Dalton often understands abstractions well when they're described computationally — in terms of inputs, outputs, states, rules, functions, and dependencies between systems. Frame explanations this way when it fits the material.

## Structured Personal Work

For structured personal work (planning, goal systems, project execution), Dalton prefers to externalize structure, define the next physical action, and work in short meaningful blocks — without lowering standards to make something feel easier.

## Writing Preferences

- Academic but practical
- Evidence-based, clear claim-support reasoning
- Precise, professional language with varied sentence structure and natural transitions
- Avoid generic corporate prose
- Avoid em dashes
- Preserve Dalton's underlying reasoning rather than replacing it with generically "polished" text that flattens what he actually meant
